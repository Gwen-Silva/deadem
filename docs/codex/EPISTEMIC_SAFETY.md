# Epistemic Safety

Separate observed facts from derived facts, candidate identities, hypotheses, and interpretation.

Do not infer:

- entity deleted = destroyed;
- entity absent/not visible = absent in game;
- zero health = killed;
- objective removed = completed;
- player near objective = contesting;
- net worth = spendable, secured, unsecured, or effective power;
- outcome favorable = decision correct.

Record uncertainty, provenance, limitations, and semantic limits. Do not create circular spatial identity: map-side anchors and replay-side coordinates must come from meaningfully separate evidence paths before fitting transforms.

Mechanic effects require version applicability plus direct state evidence. Until then, report raw state only.
